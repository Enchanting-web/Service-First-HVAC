# Service First AI-ready article guide

Use this structure for each Help Center article so customers and Zendesk AI can retrieve a direct answer:

1. **Title:** phrase the real question (for example, “Why is my air conditioner running but not cooling?”).
2. **First paragraph:** answer in plain language in 1–3 sentences; say what the customer should do next.
3. **Safe checks:** list only simple checks customers can do without opening equipment or handling wiring, refrigerant, or gas.
4. **When to stop:** state smoke, gas odor, burning smell, electrical danger, or other hazards clearly; tell customers to move to safety and contact emergency services or the utility where appropriate.
5. **When to call Service First:** give the service trigger, phone number, hours, and any appointment information as text.
6. **Related questions:** link to the most relevant follow-up articles.
7. **Maintenance:** include the publication owner and review date; update the article whenever service policy or safety guidance changes.

Keep each article focused on one issue. Spell out HVAC abbreviations on first use. Use numbered steps for a procedure, explicit units, and text descriptions for any diagram. Do not promise a diagnosis or quote without inspection. AI suggestions must preserve emergency safety instructions and should send unclear or urgent cases to a human technician.
